# BRD Discovery Questions

A **pool**, not a script. Ask 3–5 per round using `../../../shared/interview.md`: problem and outcomes first, skip anything already answered or documented, adapt to the answers, accept `Unknown` / `TBD` / `Not applicable`.

## Order of priority

1. **Problem and outcomes** — always before any solution talk
   - What is going wrong or missing today? How is it handled now?
   - Who is affected, and how often? What does it cost (money, time, risk, reputation)?
   - If this succeeded, what would be different for the business?
   - Is there a trigger or deadline that makes this urgent now?
2. **Stakeholders**
   - Who asked for this, who pays, who is affected, who must approve or comply (legal, security, operations)?
   - Who makes the final call when stakeholders disagree?
3. **Scope**
   - What must be included for this to be worth doing? What is explicitly out?
   - Are there adjacent problems people expect this to solve that it should not?
4. **Constraints and dependencies**
   - Budget, timeline, staffing limits? Regulatory or contractual obligations?
   - Systems, teams or vendors this depends on or must not disturb?
5. **Success measures**
   - How would you know it worked? Any baseline and target (or `TBD`)?
   - What would count as failure?
6. **Risks and assumptions**
   - What are you assuming that might be untrue? What could derail this?
   - What is the biggest unknown right now, and who can resolve it?

## Follow-ups triggered by answers

| If the user says… | Consider asking… |
|---|---|
| A manual process exists | Steps, volume, who does it, where it fails (→ optional *Current Process*) |
| "Regulation/compliance" | Which rules, who owns interpretation, deadlines (→ *Business Rules*) |
| A number (cost, rate, volume) | Source and date of the number; is it a target or a measurement? |
| "Everyone needs it" | Which group is first, and what breaks if the others wait? |
| A solution ("we need an app") | What problem does that solve? What else would solve it? (record the solution as a proposal, not a requirement) |
| "I don't know" | Who would know, and by when? Record as Open Question. |

## Useful option sets (suggestions only)

- Urgency: driven by a deadline / rising cost / strategic opportunity / other
- Primary benefit: cost reduction / revenue / risk or compliance / customer experience / other
- Scope posture: minimum viable first / full replacement / pilot with one group / not decided
